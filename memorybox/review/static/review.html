1. Video

Originals are read-only. Pause on a face, then create a candidate and rubber-band the face.

Video frame not decoding in this browser (audio-only / blank picture is often HEVC/H.265). Prefer an H.264/AAC copy for Review, or use a Chrome build with HEVC support. Scrubbing still needs a buffered Range-capable stream.

2. Face candidate → box → Teach

With multiple people in frame: pause, create candidate, drag a box on the one face you mean, confirm box, then Teach.

Drag a box around the face to preview the crop.
