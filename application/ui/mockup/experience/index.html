Experience gallery

Eight supporting-actor screens for philosophy validation. Visitor is the main character. MemoryBox is the curator. UI stays quiet.

EX-01 · First Five Minutes

Home / Conversation

Invitation, not a dashboard. “What would you like to explore today?”

 EX-02 · Narrative + Evidence

Understanding first

Narrative leads. Evidence is available, not the hero. Human confidence phrasing.

 EX-03 · Silence

Listening to Grandpa

Curator steps aside. Technology waits. The story has the right of way.

 EX-04 · Teach

Introduce someone

Invite, never “Unknown Person #14.” Family teaches; MemoryBox remembers.

 EX-05 · Capture

Recording a Story

Voice → transcript → human Story → People / Place / Moment linked.

 EX-06 · Stewardship

Review & Learn

Expect work; find joy. Suggestions wait for confirmation.

 EX-07 · Family Mode

Family Night

Warmer, simpler, shared. Same archive. Wonder first.

 EX-08 · Explorer Mode

Power without a new archive

Lenses and tools visible. Knowledge graph is a lens, not home.
