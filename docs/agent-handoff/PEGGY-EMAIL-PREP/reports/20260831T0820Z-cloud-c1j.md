## What changed (code at bound SHA)

1. **Present vs referenced.** `_present_own_rfcs` / `_collect_known_rfc` are Message-IDs of rows actually in the packet. `_unresolved_parent_rfcs` reports reply IDs whose parent row is absent.
2. **Fetch frontier stays own+referenced** (`_frontier_rfcs`) so parents and children are still queried.
3. **Case-safe SQL.** `lower(...)` on stored `rfc_message_id` / `message_id` / `in_reply_to` / `in_reply_to_ids` / References tokens vs the already-lowercased `ANY(%s)` keys.
4. **Queried IDs after a finished chunk.** A chunk is marked queried only when its keyset pages complete. Attach-cap mid-chunk leaves those IDs unqueried; they are merged into `unresolved_rfc_ids`.
