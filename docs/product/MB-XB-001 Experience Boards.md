## Pass / fail

1. Do I want to **spend time here**?
2. Do I understand **why each chapter followed** the last?

## Continuity rule

Every transition explains itself **through the archive**. Each chapter answers outside the frame:

| Label | Answers |
|-------|---------|
| **Happened** | What just happened |
| **Here** | Why I am seeing this / why MemoryBox brought me here |
| **Next** | What I might discover next |

Aligned with MBUX: *Why am I seeing this? Why does it matter? Where could this naturally lead?*

## Form

- Visually rich HTML atmospheres
- Outside the frame: tiny chapter title + whisper + tour bridge (Happened / Here / Next)
- Inside: people and artifacts lead; software stays quiet
- One continuous tour through a single archive (Christmas → Grandpa → Sue → watch → family)
