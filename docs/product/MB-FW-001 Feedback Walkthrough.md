## Why this exists

Experience Boards convey feeling. Prototype 1 validates interaction. Feedback sessions need **richer product moments**: typed questions, microphone, narrative + evidence counts (not files), photos of people, plain curator questions.

## Pass / fail

- Does it feel like stepping through MemoryBox — or looking at wireframes?
- Does each panel answer: Why am I seeing this? Why does it matter? Where could this lead? (MBUX)
- Would someone ask another question before the end?
